Hi !

Right, so here you are on the ‘About’ page. Here, I’m going to explain how I came up with the idea for this website, and for that we need to go back to 2017.
The day my parents dragged me and my brothers along to our very first concert: Les Cowboys Fringants. Since that day, I haven’t stopped going to them – at first always with my parents, then more recently with my friends, who I’ve had the chance to introduce to the magic of festivals. All this to say that I’ve been to LOADS of them, whether they’re concerts or festivals. I’ve always liked keeping a record of who I’ve seen or who I’d love to see, in my mobile notes (not always easy), which is why I wanted to create this website: to keep track of these precious memories.
